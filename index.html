Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

Prayer Key ("we", "our", or "the app") is a spiritual habit app that helps you build a daily prayer practice. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data stored on your device

All personal data is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate servers that store your personal information. This includes:

  • Your prayer sessions and generated prayers
  • Mood and spiritual well-being ratings
  • Your prayer streak and statistics
  • Prayer needs — titles, descriptions, and answered prayer stories you write
  • Profile information you enter during onboarding (name, age group, gender, denomination, spiritual goals, obstacles to faith, and personal struggles)
  • Bible bookmarks, highlights, and notes
  • App settings and preferences

If you delete the app, all of this data is permanently deleted from your device.

AI prayer generation

When you use AI-powered prayer generation, the app sends data to our secure proxy server. The exact data depends on which feature you use:

Standard prayer session — the following anonymised profile context is sent:

  • Your current mood rating (a number from 1–6)
  • Your current relationship-with-God rating (a number from 1–6)
  • Anonymised profile context: age group, gender, denomination, prayer frequency, spiritual goals, faith vision, obstacles to faith, and personal struggles (which may include sensitive categories such as anxiety, depression, loneliness, or addiction — selected by you during onboarding)
  • Language preference

Custom prayer — in addition to mood and basic profile (gender, age, denomination), the exact text you type as your prayer request is sent verbatim to DeepSeek AI.

Prayer need prayer — the title, description, and any current update you have written for a specific prayer need are sent verbatim to DeepSeek AI. Write only what you are comfortable sharing with an external AI service.

When using Custom Prayer or Prayer Need features, the text you type is sent as-is to DeepSeek AI. Avoid including full names, contact details, or other identifying information in your prayer request text.

Your name is never included in these requests. No account or contact information is transmitted.

All requests are routed through our Cloudflare Workers proxy, which forwards them to DeepSeek AI to generate the prayer text. Neither our proxy nor DeepSeek stores this data after the request is complete.

DeepSeek is a company based in China. Data sent for prayer generation is processed under their privacy policy and applicable Chinese law.
DeepSeek's privacy policy: deepseek.com/privacy

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare Workers — routes AI requests. Processes data in transit only; does not store personal data. Privacy policy
  • DeepSeek AI — generates prayer text based on your anonymised profile and mood. Based in China. Privacy policy

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any contact information on our servers
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs
  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not require account registration

Data shared with DeepSeek AI is used solely to generate your prayer and is not retained by us or sold to any party.

Push notifications

The app may send local push notifications to remind you to pray or pray for your saved prayer needs. These are scheduled entirely on your device — no data leaves your device for this feature. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings.

Sensitive personal information

During onboarding you may select personal struggles from a predefined list (e.g. anxiety, depression, loneliness, addiction). These selections are stored locally on your device and, if provided, are sent anonymously to DeepSeek AI as part of prayer generation to make prayers more relevant to your situation. This information is never linked to your identity and is not retained after the prayer is generated.

You provide explicit consent to this during onboarding before the app becomes active. You may reset your profile data at any time via Settings → Reset All Data.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right to erasure — all personal data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Data transiently processed by DeepSeek is not retained post-request.
  • Right to withdraw consent — you provided consent to AI data processing during onboarding. To withdraw it, stop using AI prayer generation features or reset your data via Settings.
  • Right to information — this privacy policy serves as your disclosure of how your data is used.

For questions about GDPR compliance, contact us at filyamon5@gmail.com.

Data deletion

Prayer Key: Christian Focus — developed by Nikita Raikov.

All your data is stored exclusively on your device. We do not hold any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

To delete all your data immediately:

  • Open the app
  • Go to Settings (bottom tab)
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap "Reset All Data"
  • Confirm in the dialog — all data is deleted instantly

This permanently removes: all prayer sessions, prayer needs, mood check-ins, your profile (age, denomination, goals, struggles), and all app settings.

Alternatively, uninstalling the app from your device deletes all data completely.

Data sent to DeepSeek AI for prayer generation is not stored by us or by DeepSeek after the request completes — there is nothing to delete on that end.
